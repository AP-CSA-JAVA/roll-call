Race settings

2–10 teams, 2–5 students each. Set the finish line distance.

Load the class from the quiz Sheet first (below), so each answer is matched to the right student. Each team's car moves forward by the share of its students who got it right (1 of 3 right = one third of a step). Students go to ap-csa-java.github.io/roll-call/live.html.

Teams & rosters

Upload a roster CSV to fill in every team at once, or add students one at a time below (2–5 per team). Use Δ to move a student to another team and NP to mark them not present.
